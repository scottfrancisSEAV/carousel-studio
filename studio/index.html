What the studio does

  • ✓Three carousel templates and three poster layouts, versioned
  • ✓Tenant Brand Vault — colour tokens, fonts, approved logo variants
  • ✓Binding to approved vacancy records, which lock the operational facts
  • ✓Source-checked copy assistance that no suggestion escapes without your approval
  • ✓Export to high-resolution images, PDF, and a ZIP package with a manifest
  • ✓Marketing, compliance and final review, with a publishing log

What it does not do

  • ✗Publishing to Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn — packages are posted by hand
  • ✗A live applicant-tracking-system connection — vacancy records are demonstration data
  • ✗Sign-in — anyone with this address can use the studio, and reviewer names are typed, not verified
  • ✗Shared storage — drafts, themes and logs live in this browser only

Campaign colours are working values pending the company's own brand manual, and the licence reference and compliance wording must be entered and verified before anything is published.
